THE IDEA BEHIND THE PROJECT

Engineering a greener railway.

RAIL-URJA explores the potential of converting railway vibrations into useful electrical energy. The concept brings together pressure plates, an energy conversion mechanism, storage and smart monitoring. Its long-term vision is to support suitable low-power applications while encouraging sustainable thinking.
